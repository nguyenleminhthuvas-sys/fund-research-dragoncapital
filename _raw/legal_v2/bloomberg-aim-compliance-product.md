 Compliance Solutions 
 Stay one step ahead of regulatory risk 
 Navigate the increasingly complex regulatory landscape with compliance solutions integrated across your trading and investment workflows.

 As rules evolve and regulatory scrutiny increases, firms need compliance solutions that they can use to reduce risk and lower complexity.  
 Bloomberg offers end-to-end solutions that strengthen and streamline controls at every stage of the trading and investment lifecycle. Integrated into our trusted trading and communications infrastructure, Bloomberg’s compliance tools help firms enforce mandates, prevent breaches, detect risks, and support ongoing monitoring and regulatory reporting. 
 Powered by Bloomberg data
 Our compliance solutions operate on the same high-quality, consistent market data used in Bloomberg’s trading, risk and data workflows, supporting data integrity and reducing fragmentation across functionality.  
 Tailored to your needs
 Our trading and communications compliance solutions can be easily customized to align with your firm’s policies, client mandates and regulatory obligations. 
 Built on deep regulatory expertise 
 Bloomberg actively monitors and engages with policymakers and regulators worldwide, so that our solutions can evolve alongside regulatory expectations. 
 Compliance solutions for every stage of your workflow 
 Explore our full suite of compliance solutions 
 Order modeling and handling checks 
 Embed pre-trade compliance in order modeling and handling 
 Integrate compliance constraints directly into portfolio construction and execution workflows to reduce rework, avoid late-stage friction and streamline the end-to-end process.  
 As orders are modeled or handled, we proactively flag potential rule breaches. Traders and portfolio managers receive immediate visibility into conflicts that would trigger compliance violations, allowing them to adjust intent before orders move forward.  
 Communications controls 
 Enable granular control over communication channels and interactions 
 Define and govern how users interact across Bloomberg channels, including direct communications, chatroom access, participation rules and messaging permissions. By enforcing information flow boundaries and enabling granular, policy-based controls, these tools help users with collaboration, stronger supervision and more consistent governance across Bloomberg workflows.  
 Bloomberg Vault 
 Trade compliance 
 Mitigate trading risk in real-time 
 Enforce compliance at trade entry and execution within your order management system to prevent violations pre-trade, with automated alerts enabling compliance supervisors to review and approve or reject blocked trades on an event-driven basis.  
 Because controls are embedded within the OMS workflow, compliance becomes an integrated part of the trading process, not a separate overlay.  
 Communications monitoring 
 Reduce risk at the source 
 Deploy preventive controls that warn or block non-compliant communications, including information barriers, policy-based content warnings and granular controls over how and where users communicate. For Bloomberg Message (MSG) and Instant Bloomberg (IB) data, deliver real-time compliance alerts on potential violations.  
 Bloomberg Vault 
 Record keeping 
 Configurable archiving 
 Capture and retain communications across a wide range of channels in a secure and immutable environment, which firms can set up and configure to help meet their global record-keeping obligations. 
 Bloomberg Vault 
 Communications surveillance 
 Strengthen risk detection with lexicon and AI 
 Gain cross-channel visibility into communications risk, using lexicon and AI-driven surveillance to uncover misconduct, market abuse and/or policy violations.   
 Bloomberg Vault 
 Trade surveillance 
 Enhance market abuse detection 
 Identify and investigate potential market abuse, manipulation and anomalous trading activity across asset classes with automated alert-driven workflows built into Bloomberg’s multi-asset trading intelligence tool. 
 Bloomberg Trade Surveillance 
 Best execution monitoring 
 Demonstrate and measure execution quality 
 Evaluate execution performance and demonstrate compliance with best execution obligations using analytics and benchmarking across venues, brokers and strategies. 
 BTCA 
 Regulatory reporting 
 Simplify and automate reporting workflows 
 Deliver reporting across multiple regimes and jurisdictions through a single workflow with built-in validation, eligibility checks, routing and exception management. 
 Regulatory Reporting Hub 
 End-of-day compliance checks 
 Automate end-of-day trade compliance 
 Validate firm- and portfolio-level positions at market close and manage violations with tools built into Bloomberg’s investment management and trading solutions.    This structured oversight framework supports defensible audit trails and continuous monitoring.  
 Enterprise regulatory data 
 Support compliance workflows with regulatory-ready data 
 Bloomberg's regulatory datasets help firms understand and support their global regulatory obligations. Expert-curated regulatory intelligence translates complex rules into standardized, implementation-ready data that powers scalable and auditable compliance workflows. 
 Integrate regulatory-ready data into pre-trade checks, trade compliance, monitoring, reporting and end-of-day compliance checks to reduce fragmentation, support data integrity and adapt to evolving regulatory requirements.
